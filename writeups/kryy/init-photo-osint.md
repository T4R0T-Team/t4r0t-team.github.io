## Description

We got one photograph and had to find the city, the country, and the daylight-saving time abbreviation. The image showed a long stone staircase, an old arched passage, and buildings made of light brown stone.

![The photo from the challenge: stone steps leading under an elevated arch](writeups/kryy/img/init-perugia.webp)

## Identifying the place

The architecture and the raised walkway matched the historic centre of **Perugia, Italy**, around the Via Appia / aqueduct staircase. Italy uses Central European Summer Time during daylight saving, abbreviated **CEST** (UTC+2).

## Flag

```flag
InIt{PERUGIA_ITALY_CEST}
```

Location identified. I didn't record the platform accepting it.

## What I learned

Even without GPS data, the shape of steps, bridges and buildings can be enough to narrow down a place. The last part of the question was a timezone check, not another location clue.
