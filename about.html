WHO WE ARE

T4R0T is a CTF team born in 2025 at TVETMARA Petaling Jaya (formerly known as KKTM PJ). A small crew of students who'd rather break things than just read about them: four players, four lanes, one board.

The name's a nod to tarot. A reader looks at the cards and sees what others miss. We do the same with systems.

We play CTFs locally and internationally, document every solve, and dig into real-world threats hitting Malaysia, from phishing campaigns to fresh CVEs. Everything we learn goes back to the community.

Hack to learn, not learn to hack.
